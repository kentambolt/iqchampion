[[K0215]]

Juya siffar digiri 45 a akasin agogo a kowane mataki. Launinta yana canzawa, don haka hoton na uku ya koma launin hoton farko.

[[K0216]]
Juya siffar tsakiyar fari zuwa digiri 45 a akasin agogo kuma dawo da launin duhu daga hoton farko. Zaɓi siffar mai lanƙwasa mai duhu a wannan yanayin.

[[K0217]]
Haɗa da'irar baƙi

[[K0218]]
Da'ira baƙar fata ce a sakamakon duk lokacin da da'irar da ta dace ta yi baƙi a cikin kowane shigarwar. Fari ne kawai lokacin da shigarwar biyu ke da da'irar fari a wurin.

[[K0219]]
Shigar farko tana ba da gudummawar da'irar baƙi ta ƙasa-dama; na biyu tana ba da gudummawar da'irar baƙi biyu na sama. Da'irar tsakiya da ƙasa-da-hagu suna kasancewa fari.

[[K0220]]
Faɗaɗa zuwa ƙwayoyin maƙwabta

[[[K0221]]
A kowane mataki, kowace ƙwayar shuɗi ta kasance shuɗi kuma tana ba da launi ga maƙwabtanta na kusa sama, ƙasa, hagu da dama. Faɗaɗawa tana tsayawa a iyakar grid.

[[K0222]]
Aiƙa faɗaɗawar tantanin halitta ɗaya zuwa yankin shuɗi na tsakiya. Maƙwabtan gefe da aka kai su zama shuɗi; ƙwayoyin halitta da ke nesa da su suna nan a cikin allon duba launin toka da baƙi.

[[K0223]]
Soke matsayin launin ruwan kasa mai dacewa

[[K0224]]
Kwatanta matsayin tsiri biyu na tantanin halitta biyar ta wurin matsayi. Tantanin halitta yana launin ruwan kasa a sakamakon idan daidai shigarwa ɗaya ya yi launin ruwan kasa a wurin. Daidaita ƙwayoyin launin ruwan kasa yana sokewa zuwa shuɗi.

[[K0225]]
Ruwan kasa yana faruwa a matsayi na 2, 3, 4 da 5 a tsiri na farko da kuma matsayi na 2, 3 da 5 a na biyu. Matsayi na 4 kawai ya rage launin ruwan kasa.

[[K0226]]
Cire digo ja mafi kusa

[[K0227]]
Nemo mafi ƙarancin nisa tsakanin kowace digo ja biyu. Cire kowane digo ja na biyu a wannan nisa. Sake ƙididdige mafi kusa don mataki na gaba.

[[K0228]]
A hoton tsakiya, ƙananan digogi uku ja suna shiga cikin ma'auratan diagonal mafi kusa. Cire su yana barin digo ja kawai a layi na sama, shafi na huɗu.

[[K0229]]
Matsar da launuka shida a kusa da zagaye

[[K0230]]
Karanta ƙasa tari na hagu, sannan ƙasa tari na dama. Canza kowane launi matsayi ɗaya gaba a cikin wannan zagayen matsayi shida; launin ƙasa-dama yana naɗewa zuwa saman hagu.

[[K0231]]
Daga hoton tsakiya, da'irar launin ruwan kasa ta ƙasa-dama tana naɗewa zuwa saman hagu. Tari na ƙarshe launin ruwan kasa/ja/ja a hagu da baƙi/kasa/shuɗi a dama.

[[K0232]]
Matsar da fari da baƙi a cikin kwatance daban-daban

[[K0233]]
Fari da'ira suna motsawa kusurwa ɗaya a hannun agogo kuma baƙi suna motsawa kusurwa ɗaya a hannun agogo a kowane mataki. Toka yana nufin cewa fari da baƙi suna mamaye kusurwa ɗaya; raba su kafin sake motsawa.

[[K0234]]
Hoton tsakiya ya ƙunshi fararen da'ira a kusurwoyin hagu biyu da baƙi a kusurwoyin hagu biyu da ƙasa dama. Motsa su yana ba da fari a saman hagu, launin toka a saman dama, da baƙi a kusurwoyin ƙasa biyu.

[[K0235]]

Miƙa kowane bugun kwance

[[K0236]]
A kowane mataki, miƙa kowane bugun kwance baƙi zuwa cikin ƙwayar maƙwabta a ɓangarorin biyu. Shafukan da ke akwai suna nan, kuma shafukan suna tsayawa a gefunan waje.

[[K0237]]
Miƙa bugun a cikin hoton tsakiya ƙarin ƙwayar magani ɗaya hagu da dama. Kiyaye tsayin bugun daban-daban; amsar tana nuna layukan kwance masu tsayi da suka fito a kan ƙwayoyin magani huɗu.

[[K0238]]

Girman layin triangle

[[K0239]]
Yi amfani da zane a matsayin rukuni na ƙananan alwatika. A kowane mataki, ƙara ƙaramin alwatika a wajen kowane gefen da aka fallasa, kiyaye alwatika da ke akwai da layukan haɗinsu.

[[K0240]]
Girman tsakiyar kore ta hanyar wani Layer na alwatika maƙwabta. Amsar tana riƙe da grid ɗin alwatika iri ɗaya kuma tana ƙara alwatika tare da gefunan waje.

[[K0241]]
Cire wuraren da aka yiwa alama

[[K0242]]
Digogin launin ruwan kasa suna gano wuraren da baƙar fata ke amfani da su don cirewa. Haɗa sauran wuraren da aka yi amfani da su a cikin tsari na asali. Sauran wurare biyu suna samar da sashin layi.

[[K0243]]
Cire wuraren da aka yi alama da digo-digo na sama da hagu. Ƙananan wurare biyu ne kawai suka rage, don haka amsar ita ce gajeren layin kwance kusa da ƙasan dama.

[[K0244]]

Canja ɗigo a kusurwoyin zane

[[K0245]]
Haɗa ɗigogin zane mai duhu da matsayin ɗigo 3-by-3. A kowane kusurwa, cire digon shuɗi idan yana nan, ko kuma a sanya shi idan wurin babu komai. Sauran wuraren ba za su canza ba.

[[K0246]]
Canza yanayin wurare huɗu na kusurwoyin siffar da ke a karkace. Sakamakon zai kasance yana da dige-dige masu launin shuɗi a: saman hagu da tsakiyar sama, tsakiyar tsakiya da tsakiyar dama, da kuma dukkan wurare uku na ƙasa.

[[K0247]]
Cire tubalin da aka yi wa alama da kuma tubalan da ke kusa da shi.

[[K0248]]
Da farko, cire tubalin ja da duk tubalan da ke haɗe da shi (ta gefe ɗaya). Bayan haka, bar tubalan da ba su da abin da ke tallafa musu su faɗi ƙasa kai tsaye. Maimaita wannan tsari ga tubalin mai launin rawaya.
