Privacy policy

IQ Champion is a free visual reasoning challenge for entertainment. Its estimated IQ result is not a clinical or verified assessment.

Who is responsible?

IQ Champion is operated by Kent Caspersen. You can contact us at contact@iqchampion.com. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use our feedback and contact form. No account is required.

Your test stays on your device

Your answers, original start time, deadline, question times, result and preferred language are saved in your browser's local storage. They let you resume after closing or reloading the app. These answers and results are not automatically uploaded. Clear this site's browser data to remove them. On Android, web storage is managed by the browser that powers the app; uninstalling the launcher may leave browser data in place.

Usage and technical data

We use self-hosted Umami analytics and a service on munthesystems.dk to understand page visits, general interactions, test completion and technical failures. Requests include IP address and browser information. These can be used to estimate country, device type and a short-lived analytics session. We do not request GPS, contacts, camera, microphone or an advertising ID.

Starting a test sends a random event identifier and the start timestamp to an operational notification service. It sends no answers or IQ result. An undelivered event can remain in local IndexedDB for retry for up to seven days. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable our analytics and test-start notifications. The test remains usable.

Optional feedback and sharing

If you submit feedback, we receive the message and any name or email you choose to provide, so we can respond. Avoid including sensitive information. Choosing Facebook sharing opens Facebook with the challenge or result link you selected; Facebook then handles that interaction under its own privacy policy. Sharing does not happen automatically.

The current public version does not collect your age, country choice, individual answers or question timings for a comparison database. If this changes, the feature and policy will explain the choice before collection begins.

Providers, security and retention

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Operational services and private feedback are hosted on Hetzner in the EU. These providers process connection data needed to deliver and protect the service. Our own API traffic uses HTTPS; feedback content is encrypted at rest and access is restricted. We do not sell your data or use it for personalised advertising.

The technical event journal is retained for up to 30 days, feedback for up to 90 days and notification records for up to 90 days. Aggregate analytics may be kept longer to track product usage. Encrypted backup copies have a 14-day retention period. Locally saved attempts remain until you clear site data or the app replaces the saved attempt.

Your choices

You can use the test without sending feedback or sharing. Enable your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to opt out of our usage collection. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted. We may need enough information to locate and verify your request; we cannot identify your local test answers on our servers.
